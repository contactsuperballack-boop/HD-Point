> **Key takeaways**
>
> - Dubai has launched the Create AI Agents Championship, a global competition with a prize pool of more than AED 2.5 million. [[c1]](#src-c1)
> - The Crown Prince of Dubai invited innovators from across the world. [[c2]](#src-c2)
> - There are four core categories, and participation is free of charge. [[c5]](#src-c5)[[c12]](#src-c12)
> - Teams build an AI agent prototype, a pitch deck and a demo video, and 24 teams move on to the semi-final. [[c9]](#src-c9)

## Why it matters

The organiser says the championship shifts the focus from developing digital applications to creating autonomous AI-agent systems built for real user needs and market requirements. [[c3]](#src-c3)

It is part of the Create Apps in Dubai initiative, led by the Dubai Chamber of Digital Economy to support app development, digital skills and technology entrepreneurship. [[c4]](#src-c4)

## What it offers

### Four categories

- Best Societal AI Agent, which recognises agents that strengthen social cohesion and inclusion and support education, sustainability and social resilience. [[c6]](#src-c6)
- Best Business AI Agent, which recognises agents that help public and private organisations improve workflows, services, decision-making and customer experience. [[c7]](#src-c7)
- Best Personal AI Agent and Best Youth AI Agent complete the four core categories. [[c5]](#src-c5)

### Prizes and format

One of the four category winners will also receive the title of Dubai AI Agent of the Year. [[c8]](#src-c8) Participants create an AI agent prototype, a pitch deck and a demo video, and 24 teams move forward to pitch in the semi-final. [[c9]](#src-c9)

### Support for builders

The Digital Academy offers 10 short videos that explain AI agents without requiring a technical background. [[c10]](#src-c10) Participants also get access to workshops, specialised sessions and mentorship. [[c11]](#src-c11)

## How to enter

Participation in the championship is free of charge. [[c12]](#src-c12)
