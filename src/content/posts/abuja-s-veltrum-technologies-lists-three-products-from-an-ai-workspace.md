## What it does

### Afro AI

Afro AI is described as a document-grounded workspace for conversation, learning and organisational knowledge. [[c4]](#src-c4)

### Accessum

Accessum is described as guest and security management designed for gated communities. [[c5]](#src-c5)

### GetElectric

GetElectric is described as a direct electricity purchase experience for quick, everyday transactions. [[c6]](#src-c6)

## Who is behind it

The site names seven team members, five of them as co-founders. [[c8]](#src-c8)
