## What it does

Ignite lists six services in all, each offered on demand: SI products, data, experts, evaluation, benchmark and intelligence. [[c3]](#src-c3)

### Training

On training, the company says Ignite Academy develops SI-native experts, with capstone projects and portfolios as part of the programme. [[c6]](#src-c6)

## Who is behind it

Ignite says it is part of Trustbridge Corp, which provides its ownership, governance and long-term direction. [[c5]](#src-c5)
