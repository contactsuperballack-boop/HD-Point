## Why it matters

Smartel says its focus is helping vulnerable communities in climate-arid regions grow food more sustainably. [[c3]](#src-c3) It adds that its work aligns with the UN Sustainable Development Goals, including food security, water conservation and climate resilience. [[c5]](#src-c5)

## What it does

The company says it uses hydroponics, which deliver nutrients directly through water, so that crops can be grown without soil. [[c1]](#src-c1) It claims its systems use up to 90% less water than traditional farming. [[c2]](#src-c2)

## Business model

For farmers, Smartel says it offers a 70/30 revenue-sharing model. [[c4]](#src-c4)
