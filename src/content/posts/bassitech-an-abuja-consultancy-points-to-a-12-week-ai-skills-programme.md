## What it does

The company says it serves businesses and organisations in fintech, telecoms, agriculture and media. [[c2]](#src-c2)

### Digital Wings Initiative

BassiTech says the initiative is an AI-focused digital skills programme in Taraba State. [[c3]](#src-c3) It is designed for 100 young people [[c4]](#src-c4) and runs for 12 weeks. [[c5]](#src-c5)

### Other work

The company says it has designed 2 farming programmes for Kudan LGA, [[c6]](#src-c6) and its site lists 5 projects and ventures in total. [[c7]](#src-c7)

## Who is behind it

BassiTech says it is led by founder Anas Abubakar Bassi, a Computer Engineer who studied at Ahmadu Bello University, Zaria. [[c8]](#src-c8)
